04 — Research experience

Research in practice.

Research roles and technical contributions, based on my résumé.

Jun 2026 — Present

Graduate Student Researcher

Cybersecurity Lab · IIT Patna, Bihar

Developing an AI-driven insulin pump security digital twin that couples SimGlucose-based physiological simulation with ML/DL glucose prediction and GECO-based physiological modeling to establish patient-specific safety expectations.

Integrating hazard and survival analysis with reachability-based safety constraints to assess emerging risks, while cyberattack detection and evidence fusion inform risk-aware decisions for insulin delivery monitoring and safety responses.

Building an NVIDIA Omniverse/OpenUSD interface to visualize patient physiology, pump behavior, twin predictions, and security decisions in real time.

Jul 2025 — Jun 2026

Research & Development Intern

CVPR Lab · IIT Patna, Bihar

Conducted research focusing on temporal recommender systems, evolving user preferences, GloVe-based semantic representations, behavioral drift analysis, and predictive modeling using Random Forest, PCA, K-Means, and DBSCAN.

Developed TrustShield-VLM, an OpenCLIP-based semantic integrity framework, analyzing 156 image-text samples through 512-dimensional embeddings and a 1,560-edge trust graph, integrating six indicators to assess multimodal representation reliability.
